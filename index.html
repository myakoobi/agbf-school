Our Mission

Education is a Right, Not a Privilege

Afghan Girls Bright Future (AGBF) was founded to ensure that no girl's education ends because of geography, conflict, or circumstance. We deliver rigorous, caring online schooling to girls across Afghanistan and the diaspora who have been denied access to classrooms.

Our teachers, curriculum designers, and mentors believe that an educated generation of Afghan women will shape a better world — for Afghanistan and beyond.
